How do I play?

Tap a free tile to move it up to the tray. When two matching tiles meet in the tray, they disappear. A tile is free when nothing is on top of it and its left or right side is open. Clear all tiles to finish the level and reveal a new travel photo.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Use Hint to see a matching pair, Undo to take back a move, or Shuffle to mix the remaining tiles. Every puzzle can be solved. You can also tap a tile in the tray to put it back on the board.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.
