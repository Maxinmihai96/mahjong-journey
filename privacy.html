Privacy Policy

Mahjong Journey: Big Tiles · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Mahjong Journey: Big Tiles ("the app") is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle game. We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app uses.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

Game progress

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, daily puzzle history and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, this data is deleted too.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The current version of the app does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it does not share any data with third parties. If a future version adds features such as advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Photos

The travel photos shown in the game are provided by photographers on Unsplash under the Unsplash License.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
